Shared albums

The TV can show an album you have shared online, and keeps it up to date.

Immich: how to get the link

Open the album in Immich, tap Share, choose “Create link” and copy it.

Google Photos: how to get the link

Open the album, tap Share, choose “Create link” and copy it. Only albums shared by link work.

iCloud Shared Albums: how to get the link

In Photos, open the Shared Album, tap the people icon, turn on “Public Website”, then “Share Link” and copy it.

    How it works and privacy

    Photos go straight from your phone to the TV over your home Wi-Fi and are stored on the TV. Nothing is uploaded to the internet. The TV reads album links itself. There is no account and nothing to install.
